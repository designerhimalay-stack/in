Typical engagements

What a typical engagement looks like.

Three patterns we see with Indian businesses, and how we approach them.

A mid-sized manufacturer

Scattered documents and slow approvals were delaying deliveries. We cleaned up and migrated ten years of files, set up a SharePoint intranet, and automated approvals through central document libraries.

A logistics enterprise

COBOL-based software caused frequent downtime and limited visibility. We re-engineered and moved the legacy applications to Azure, simplified the back-end data, and added cloud storage that scales.

A fintech company

Unpredictable customer behaviour made campaigns and launches hard to plan. We built custom AI models on Azure AI services, with automated dashboards for customer insight.
