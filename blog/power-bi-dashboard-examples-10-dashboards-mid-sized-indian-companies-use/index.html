Power BI Dashboard Examples: 10 Dashboards Mid-Sized Indian Companies Use

Ten Power BI dashboards mid-sized Indian companies use, from sales and finance to operations, and what each one shows.

The Power BI dashboards that stick in mid-sized Indian companies are the ones that replace the monthly Excel MIS. Finance usually starts with cash flow and GST reconciliation. Plants follow with inventory and output views. Every one of them needs the April to March financial year and figures in lakh and crore built in, or people drift back to Excel within a month.

I see the same file in almost every first meeting. It’s called something like MIS_Sept_final_v7.xlsx, one accounts executive spends three days on it after month-end close, and it reaches the managing director on a Friday. Nobody doubts the numbers. They just arrive two weeks after anyone could act on them. [CONFIRM AUTHOR EXPERIENCE]

Which Power BI dashboards do mid-sized Indian companies build first?

Most start with the CFO’s cash and receivables dashboard. The data already sits in Tally or the ERP, and the payoff shows up in the first collections review. Plant and inventory dashboards usually come second because they need cleaner master data and a gateway to on-premises systems.

The 10 Power BI dashboard examples, one by one

1. CFO cash flow and receivables dashboard

Receivables ageing is where most finance teams want to begin. Overdue amounts sit by customer and by ageing bucket, from current through to over 90 days, with the cash position across your bank accounts beside them.

The page that changes behavior is the collector view. Each person sees only their own customers, sorted by the overdue amount in lakh. My advice is to build this one first: the data is already in your books, and the CFO will open it in the Monday review without being asked.

2. GST reconciliation dashboard

Every month someone in accounts downloads GSTR-2B from the GST portal and matches it against the purchase register in Excel, line by line. Power BI can do that match on the supplier’s GSTIN and invoice number.

What finance actually uses is one short page. It lists suppliers whose invoices are in your books but missing from their filings, with the input tax credit at stake for each. That list goes to the purchase team to chase before the return is due. [VERIFY LOCAL DETAIL]

3. Sales and dealer performance dashboard

Manufacturers that sell through distributors across states live with two sales numbers. Primary sales to the distributor look healthy long before secondary sales to retailers tell you whether stock is moving. A useful sales dashboard shows both by state and by dealer, against the monthly target.

Here’s the catch. Secondary sales often arrive as Excel files shared on WhatsApp, each in its own layout. Agree a simple upload template with distributors before anyone designs a chart, because the dashboard is only as current as the slowest file.

4. Inventory and stock ageing dashboard

Ask a supply chain head where working capital is stuck and they’ll point at the warehouse. This dashboard shows stock value by plant and warehouse, how long each item has sat, and which items haven’t moved in 180 days. Reorder levels sit on the same page.

Units cause more trouble than anything else. When one item is stored in kilograms at one plant and in bags at another, every total is wrong until the item master is fixed.

5. Plant production and downtime dashboard

A plant head in Pune doesn’t need a month-end report on output. They need yesterday’s shift, by line, with the downtime reasons supervisors logged. The dashboard compares planned against actual output for every line and shift.

Many plants add overall equipment effectiveness as a single score per line. Treat it with care. If downtime reasons are written on paper at the end of the shift and typed in the next morning, the score will look better than the shop floor does.

6. Procurement and vendor performance dashboard

Purchase heads judge vendors on two questions: did the material arrive on time, and did it pass inspection? The dashboard links each purchase order to its goods receipt note. Quality rejections are then matched to the same receipts.

Lead time and rejection rate show up per vendor, per quarter. The annual vendor review stops being a matter of opinion.

7. Budget vs actual dashboard

In April, every department head agrees a budget. By October, few of them can say where they stand without asking accounts. This dashboard shows budget against actual by cost centre for each month of the financial year, with year-to-date variance in lakh.

Build the April to March year into a custom date table on day one. The default calendar runs January to December, and every quarter comparison will be wrong without it.

8. Workforce and contract labour dashboard

Plants with a large contract workforce often carry two headcounts that never match: the one in the attendance system and the one on the contractor’s bill. A workforce dashboard puts them side by side for each plant and contractor.

Personal data needs care here. Use row-level security so each plant head sees only their own site, and keep employee-level detail out of widely shared reports. That also helps with your obligations under the Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023.

9. Quality and customer complaints dashboard

Quality heads usually have the data and no time to read it. Inspection rejections live in one register and customer complaints in another.

Linking the two by product and batch shows whether a complaint spike started on the shop floor two weeks earlier. That trail is what large customers ask for during supplier audits.

10. Microsoft cloud and licence cost dashboard

IT heads get asked about the Azure bill every quarter, usually by the CFO. Azure Cost Management exports daily cost by subscription, and with consistent tags Power BI can show spend by department or project.

Add Microsoft 365 licence assignments to the same model. You’ll often find paid licences still sitting on accounts of people who left months ago.

See your first dashboard on your own data

Start with cash and receivables, built on the Tally or ERP data you already have. If the numbers match your month-end MIS, move on to the next dashboard. If they don’t, you’ll know exactly where the data needs work before spending more.

What do Indian finance teams need set up in Power BI?

Three settings decide whether finance trusts the numbers. Get them right in the data model before anyone builds a visual.

  1. A financial-year date table. Quarters should read Q1 for April to June. DAX year-to-date functions such as TOTALYTD accept a year-end date, so pass “3/31”.
  2. Lakh and crore figures. Power BI’s built-in display units are thousands, millions, and billions. Create measures that divide by 1,00,000 or 1,00,00,000 and label the axis “₹ lakh” or “₹ crore”.
  3. A gateway to on-premises data. Tally and many ERPs run on a server in the office. Power BI reaches them through the on-premises data gateway, and TallyPrime can expose data over ODBC. Keep the gateway machine switched on and patched, or the 9 a.m. refresh fails without anyone noticing until the review.

Refresh limits matter too. With Power BI Pro, a semantic model (formerly called a dataset) on shared capacity can refresh on schedule up to eight times a day. Premium Per User and Fabric capacity raise that to 48 (Microsoft Learn, 2025). For a plant shift view, eight is usually enough. A live dispatch board is a different conversation.

How long does a Power BI dashboard take, and what does it cost?

A first dashboard on reasonably clean data usually takes two to four weeks. Plant and inventory dashboards that need data cleanup or a gateway take four to eight. Cost depends far more on the state of your source data than on how many visuals you want.

Licensing is the other line item. Anyone viewing a dashboard shared from a Pro workspace needs a Power BI Pro licence, unless the content sits on a large enough Fabric capacity. Check your Microsoft 365 plan before buying anything: Microsoft 365 E5 already includes Power BI Pro. [VERIFY CURRENT MICROSOFT LICENSING]

For a CFO, the useful comparison is the time the MIS takes today. If an accounts executive spends three days on it every month-end, that is the work the first dashboard should retire.

Where Power BI dashboards don’t help

A dashboard can’t fix numbers that are wrong at the source. If three people keep their own copies of the customer master, the receivables view will show three versions of the same distributor, and finance will stop trusting it inside a week. Fix the master data first, even if it pushes the dashboard back by a month.

Dashboards also only show what has already happened. Next quarter’s demand or cash position needs a forecasting model on top, which is a separate piece of work.

How to choose a Power BI consulting company in India

Pick a team that asks about your data before it shows you sample visuals. A good Power BI consultant asks to see your current MIS file in the first conversation, then asks who signs off the numbers.

Use these questions to compare Power BI consulting companies:

  1. Have they built an April to March date table and lakh and crore measures before? Ask to see one.
  2. Will they connect to your real sources, including Tally or an on-premises ERP, through a gateway?
  3. Do they set up row-level security and workspace access, or just hand over a .pbix file?
  4. Who owns the semantic model after go-live, and will your team be trained to change it?
  5. Will they prove it on one dashboard with your data before you commit to ten?

Frequently asked questions

What are the most useful Power BI dashboard examples for finance teams?

Cash flow and receivables, budget vs actual, and GST reconciliation are the three most finance teams in India build first. All three use data already in Tally or the ERP, so they can go live in two to four weeks.

Can Power BI connect to Tally?

Yes. TallyPrime can expose data over ODBC, and Power BI reaches it through the on-premises data gateway. The machine running Tally and the gateway must be on at refresh time.

How do I show values in lakh and crore in Power BI?

Power BI’s built-in display units are thousands, millions, and billions. Create measures that divide by 1,00,000 for lakh or 1,00,00,000 for crore, and label the axis or card with the unit.

How much does a Power BI consultant cost in India?

Cost depends mainly on how clean your source data is and how many sources the dashboard pulls from, not on the number of charts. Ask for a fixed scope on one dashboard first, so you can judge the work before a larger commitment.

How long does it take to build a Power BI dashboard?

A first dashboard on clean data usually takes two to four weeks. Dashboards that need data cleanup or a connection to on-premises systems take four to eight weeks.

Does everyone who views a dashboard need a Power BI Pro licence?

In a Pro workspace, yes, every viewer needs Pro. Content on a large enough Fabric capacity can be viewed with free licences. Check your Microsoft 365 plan first, since Microsoft 365 E5 includes Power BI Pro.
